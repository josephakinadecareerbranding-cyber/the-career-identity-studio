Post 02 · Educational

Three signs your CV is underselling you

1. It lists duties but not outcomes. Employers need to understand the contribution behind the task.
2. It uses the same language for every opportunity. Your strongest evidence should connect to the role you want.
3. It is difficult to scan. Clear structure helps a reader find relevant experience quickly.

A stronger CV does not need exaggerated claims. It needs specific evidence, thoughtful prioritisation and clear writing.

What is one part of your CV you would improve today?

#CVTips #JobSearch #CareerGrowth

Post 03 · LinkedIn profile

Your LinkedIn headline should do more than repeat your job title.

A useful headline can communicate your area of expertise, the problems you help solve and the value you bring.

Instead of relying only on a title, consider a structure like:
Role / specialism | Core expertise | Value or industry focus

Keep it truthful, specific and aligned with the opportunities you want to attract.

#LinkedInTips #ProfessionalBranding #PersonalBrand
