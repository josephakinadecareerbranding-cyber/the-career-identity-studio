What shapes your quotation?

  • The service or combination of services required.
  • Your career level, industry and target roles.
  • The amount of source material and research needed.
  • Whether a specific job description or target opportunity is available.
  • Your deadline and any agreed additional revisions.

How to begin

  1. Send a brief enquiry describing your career goal and requested service.
  2. We review the scope and may ask for relevant context or existing documents.
  3. You receive a quotation, deliverables and estimated timeline for approval.
  4. Work begins after scope, payment arrangements and required information are agreed.
  5. We share the draft for the agreed review and refinement process.

What to prepare

  • Your current CV or LinkedIn URL, if relevant.
  • A target job description or role direction, if available.
  • A short note about your goals, achievements and deadline.
  • Any specific preferences or requirements.
