A few helpful answers

Frequently asked questions.

Who do you work with?

We support people at all career levels, including students and graduates, career changers, early- and mid-career professionals, managers and senior leaders.

Will my resume be tailored to a specific role?

Yes. Where a target role or job description is available, we use it to guide the language, priorities and evidence presented in your career documents.

How does the process begin?

Send an enquiry describing your goals and the service you need. We will discuss scope, timelines, required information and the next steps before work begins.

Do you guarantee interviews or job offers?

No. We improve the clarity and quality of your career materials and job-search approach, but hiring decisions are made by employers and cannot be guaranteed.

How do you handle my information?

Career documents can contain personal information. We treat client materials as confidential and use them only for the agreed service. Please share only information necessary to begin the project.
